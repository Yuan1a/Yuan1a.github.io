This paper studies a **non-contact 3D deformation measurement** technique based on **laser speckle**:

* A high-precision acquisition platform built with 20MP binocular industrial cameras and a customized matrix speckle laser, capturing ambient-light-resistant laser speckle images.
* Speckle extraction with OpenCV; bilinear interpolation to eliminate speckle connectivity issues; multi-point three-axis coordinates solved with the similar-triangle method; planar 3D reconstruction visualized with pseudo-color maps.
* Achieved 0.02mm measurement accuracy in X/Y directions, and Z-direction accuracy better than 0.01mm on 80% of measured points.
