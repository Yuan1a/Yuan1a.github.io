This study tackles weakly supervised 3D prostate MRI segmentation with only a few advanced prompt points instead of dense pixel-level annotations:

* An annotation scheme based on **seed clusters (advanced prompt points)** — one foreground seed point plus six edge relaxation points — to generate pseudo-labels.
* The **ACEA-Net** weakly supervised semantic learning segmentation framework, which solves the under-expansion problem caused by the lack of semantic affinity of seed-point pixels during pseudo-label generation, leveraging **geodesic distance transform**.

The method completes 3D prostate MRI segmentation with minimal annotation cost while maintaining competitive accuracy.
