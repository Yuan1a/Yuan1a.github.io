This work proposes a **patch-based plug-and-play correction guidance** strategy for semi-supervised prostate multi-regional (sub-regional) semantic segmentation:

* A small number of labeled samples are used to **correct the pseudo-labels** of unlabeled samples.
* The correction module is designed as a **general plug-and-play plugin** that can be embedded into existing semi-supervised segmentation networks to improve performance.

*Status: under review (first round) at a CAS Top journal.*
